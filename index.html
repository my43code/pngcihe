Faith-driven excellence

Preparing servant leaders for Port Moresby and the Highlands region.

40+ years of Catholic education

Empowering PNG students to transform communities

PNGC Institute of Higher Education (PNGC IHE) is a ministry of the Catholic Archdiocese of Port Moresby and the University of Notre Dame Australia partnership. We combine strong academics, pastoral formation and community engagement so every student can serve with integrity, innovation and compassion.

  • Accredited by DHERST & Catholic Education Agency
  • Digital-first classrooms and collaborative studios
  • Spiritual direction & service-learning each semester

Campus life snapshot

Beyond lectures, PNGC IHE students join choirs, sports, Caritas outreaches and Port Moresby youth conventions. Our residential chaplaincy team hosts weekly formation sessions, counselling, and retreats to nurture wholeness.
