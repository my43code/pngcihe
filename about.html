Who we are

About PNGC Institute of Higher Education

PNGC IHE exists to develop Catholic leaders who respond courageously to the social, economic and spiritual needs of Papua New Guinea. Our integrated curriculum balances academic rigor, pastoral care and Melanesian cultural intelligence.

Explore our mission

Institutional identity

We are chartered by the Catholic Archdiocese of Port Moresby and aligned with DHERST, TVET and partner universities across the Pacific. Our culture is rooted in stewardship, hospitality and excellence.

Holistic formation

We nurture intellectual curiosity, spiritual growth and professional competence through pastoral accompaniment and peer mentoring groups.

  • Weekly house meetings and chaplaincy support
  • Ignatian-style reflection and journaling
  • Community-based research in Port Moresby parishes

Industry partnerships

Our memoranda of understanding with Catholic Health Services, National Capital District Commission and private sector enterprises ensure authentic work-integrated learning.

  • Clinical placements in rural aid posts
  • Entrepreneurship boot camps with SMEs
  • Joint teacher training with Port Moresby Catholic College

Quality assurance

We benchmark against regional universities, complete annual program reviews and publish transparent data on graduate destinations.

  • Annual external moderation
  • Digital learning analytics
  • Alumni tracer studies

Milestones

1981 – Founding

PNGC IHE opened as a teacher training centre beside the Our Lady of Fatima parish to meet Port Moresby's urgent need for qualified educators.

1998 – University affiliation

Signed partnership with the University of Notre Dame Australia to align curriculum, supervision and credit transfer pathways.

2015 – Campus expansion

Construction of the Learning Commons, digital studio and women’s residence enabled a 35% increase in enrolment.

2023 – Innovation hub

Launched the Port Moresby Innovation Hub, connecting alumni entrepreneurs, microfinance groups and student researchers.

Values in action

Faith & Integrity

Daily prayer, Eucharist and counselling ensure students discern their vocation and lead with honesty.

Excellence & Innovation

Project-based learning, innovation sprints and peer observation drive continuous improvement.

Community & Care

We prioritise trauma-informed support, community development projects and family-inclusive education plans.

Stewardship

Solar-powered dorms, water harvesting and sustainable agriculture plots teach environmental responsibility.

Meet our community

Students represent 17 provinces, bringing languages, customs and talents that enrich our learning community. Tutors and chaplains accompany students individually through growth plans.
