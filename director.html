Director's Message

It is my privilege to welcome you to PNGC Institute of Higher Education, where we champion inclusive and industry-ready learning. I invite you to read the message below, which reflects our shared commitment to excellence, innovation, and community impact.

Dr. Naida Waine

Director, PNGC Institute of Higher Education

A Vision Rooted in Service

Every learner who walks through our doors carries a story, a dream, and a profound responsibility to serve their communities. At PNGC Institute of Higher Education, we nurture those stories by combining academic rigor with practical exposure so graduates remain grounded, confident, and ready for the future.

As Director, I am inspired daily by the resilience of our students and staff. Together, we are building a campus culture that celebrates curiosity, champions collaboration, and aligns learning with national development priorities. Our laboratories, studios, and learning hubs are designed as places where bold ideas can be tested and refined.

“We strive to graduate professionals who not only master their disciplines but also possess the empathy and leadership to transform the communities they serve.”

I extend my gratitude to parents, industry partners, and alumni who continue to place their trust in us. Your encouragement enables us to expand pathways into health, education, technology, and business sectors across Papua New Guinea. Let us continue to stand together, share knowledge generously, and create opportunities for the next generation.

Thank you for being part of the PNGC Institute family.

With respect and determination,
Dr. Naida Waine
