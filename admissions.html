Admissions 2025

Join a community committed to faith and service

Whether you are a Grade 12 school leaver, a working professional or a sponsored trainee, PNGC IHE provides flexible pathways, bridging courses and scholarship support.

Application steps DHERST portal

How to apply

Step 1

Create your profile

Submit your expression of interest via DHERST NOAS or download the PNGC IHE form if you are a non-school leaver.

Download PDF
Step 2

Upload documents

Attach certified copies of Grade 10 & 12 certificates, birth certificate, NID and sponsor letter (if applicable).

Step 3

Interview & assessments

Shortlisted applicants complete an online literacy test and a values interview with our faculty panel.

Step 4

Offer & onboarding

Offers are issued via email and SMS. Accepted students receive onboarding packs, reading lists and pre-orientation modules.

Entry requirements

School leavers

  • Grade 12 certificate with minimum B in English and Mathematics
  • Letter of recommendation from parish priest or school principal
  • Participation in community service documented in CV

Non-school leavers

  • Evidence of Grade 12 or equivalent upgrading
  • Two years of work or ministry experience
  • Supervisor reference and police clearance

International & exchange

  • IELTS 6.0 or letter of English proficiency
  • Passport biodata page and travel insurance
  • Visa recommendation from PNG Immigration

Scholarships & financial aid

Diocesan sponsorships

Parishes may nominate catechists or teachers for partial tuition support. Contact the Catholic Education Agency for guidelines.

STEM sisters fund

Provides laptops and tuition subsidies for young women pursuing ICT or science education pathways.

Community service bursary

Students who commit to rural practicum placements receive transport allowances and mentorship grants.
